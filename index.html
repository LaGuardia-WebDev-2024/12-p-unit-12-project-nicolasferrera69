  2. AI Piece for Digital Media

    My illustration shows a monstrous AI hand crushing a ball in a traditional still life, symbolizing how AI is killing traditional art and the practice of making art by hand. The cracks in the ball mirror the shapes of Earth's continents, hinting that AI's damage will spread far beyond the art world. Overheating computer servers use huge amounts of electricity and release carbon emissions that harm our atmosphere. They also waste millions of gallons of water for cooling, draining natural resources. We must regulate tech companies before AI destroys the natural systems all life depends on.
